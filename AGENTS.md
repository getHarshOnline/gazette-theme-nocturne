# Working on Nocturne

Read [WORLD.md](WORLD.md) for the accepted composition and its unfinished motifs, then [Compositions](docs/compositions.md) for the selectors and source bindings. Apply `gazette-theme-base`’s `AGENTS.md` for shared behavior, ownership, neutral fixtures and verification. Pale’s [design guidance](https://github.com/getHarshOnline/pale/blob/main/packages/design/INDEX.md) gives the method, and its [browser task](https://github.com/getHarshOnline/pale/blob/main/packages/design/browser-task.md) page gives the browser review route.

This repository owns Nocturne’s presentation. `design-tokens` owns values, Base owns shared interaction, and the composing workspace selects the site, content and assets. Follow the [build and check route](README.md#build-and-check); change token values at their producer and recapture them rather than editing generated files.

Preserve the painted passage and the full native storyboard. Existing `painted-*` names are identifiers only and do not select an earlier opening composition. Scene records own anatomy, geography and the edit; the adapters use Base’s player. Keep inquiry essays in Reading until a chapter-specific selector exists. Dark is the default, with a complete light expression.

Keep the world recognisable without accent, and check held light and reading contrast in both modes. Fixtures use fictional content; presentation must preserve facts, units, status, sources and destinations. If the engine cannot carry an approved treatment, report the gap to Base or Gazette’s owner and retain the treatment pending that decision. Implementation and passing checks do not constitute design approval. Work and commit in this repository; the workspace decides what renders.
